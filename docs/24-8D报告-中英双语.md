## D4 根因分析

### 4.1 已证实的失效链（证据：18 台失效马达电测普查 + 17 块失效板器件解剖 + 温箱复现）

1. **绕组绝缘局部失效引发电弧**，两种结局：细线（Φ0.16 mm）被电弧**熔断 = 开路**（18 台中 11 台，线包外观正常，对应"异味"类失效）；或被电弧**焊连 = 短路**（7 台，其中 6 台呈现"单相恰好损失一只齿线圈（−3 Ω）"的一致签名，对应短路级联止步于线圈骨架边界）；
2. **短路型事件驱动大电流流经逆变桥**：IPM 结温高时，器件短路耐受能量塌缩/寄生管闩锁 → 硅片失效为短路（温箱实验证实温度决定失效烈度；现场"安静:爆响 ≈ 2:1"与之一致）；
3. **母线电容组（4×47 µF/400 V）经失效硅片放电**：高压端储能 ~11–13 J（低压端仅 ~1.9 J）→ 封装爆裂 = 爆响。器件解剖显示"驱动芯片+上管跨多相连坐"的单次剧烈事件签名；真直通签名仅 1/17，排除直通为主因。

### 4.2 引发点根因（最可能，六条独立证据线支持；物理定位验证进行中）

**槽内绕组-铁芯绝缘薄弱点（绕线应力/绝缘空隙），仅在高电压下被电激活。**

| # | 证据线 | 内容 |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | 电压选择性 | 17:1 失效比，低压机群 22,480 台样本充分，非曝光量假象 |
| E2 | 对机壳绝缘塌方 | 9/18 失效马达绕组对机壳绝缘降至 ≤360 kΩ（最低 400 Ω）；一台 500 V 耐压通过、800 V 击穿（规格 1600 VAC）|
| E3 | 物理阈值 | 绕组对铁芯承受 ~170–373 V PWM 阶跃（高压端），超过气隙放电最低点火电压（约 330 V，Paschen）；低压端 ≤170 V **物理上无法点燃**——与 17:1 二值型选择性吻合 |
| E4 | 时间尺度 | PWM 边沿 ~10⁵ 次/秒 × 漆膜放电耐受 10⁹–10¹⁰ 次 = **3–30 小时**，与 ≤5 小时现场失效吻合；完好漆膜纯热失效需热点 ~240 °C（Arrhenius），会呈大面积碳化，与"线包外观正常"矛盾——排除纯热老化 |
| E5 | 湿度梯度 | 高压地区内失效率随气候湿度单调排序（SG>CN>HK>KR>干燥地区 0），与放电/爬电的湿度敏感性一致（出货时间归一化验证进行中）|
| E6 | 几何汇合 | 6 槽集中绕组每槽容纳两个不同相线圈+铁芯，槽口为绕线机械应力集中处——与"单线圈损失+多相对地劣化"的观测吻合 |

## D4 Root Cause Analysis

### 4.1 Confirmed failure sequence (evidence: electrical survey of 18 failed motors + component forensics of 17 failed boards + chamber reproduction)

1. **A localized winding-insulation failure ignites an arc**, with two endpoints: the Φ0.16 mm wire **fuses open** (11/18 units; winding visually normal — the "burning smell" mode), or **welds short** (7/18; six of seven show an identical signature — one phase losing exactly one tooth-coil, −3 Ω — arc cascade bounded by the bobbin);
2. **Short-type events drive fault current through the inverter**: at elevated IPM junction temperature the device's short-circuit withstand collapses / parasitic latch fires → die fails short (chamber tests confirm temperature governs failure violence; field quiet-to-pop ratio ≈2:1 is consistent);
3. **The bus capacitor bank (4×47 µF/400 V) discharges through the failed die**: ~11–13 J stored at high line (vs ~1.9 J at low line) → package rupture = pop. Board forensics show a "driver-die + high-side MOSFET damaged across multiple phases" signature of a single violent bus event; a true shoot-through signature appears in only 1/17 — excluded as a primary cause.

### 4.2 Most probable initiation root cause (supported by six independent evidence lines; physical confirmation in progress)

**A winding-to-core insulation weak point inside the stator slot (winding-process mechanical stress / insulation voids), electrically activated only at high line voltage.**

| # | Evidence line | Content |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Voltage selectivity | 17:1 failure ratio with a statistically sufficient low-voltage fleet (22,480 units) — not an exposure artifact |
| E2 | Winding-to-core insulation collapse | 9/18 failed motors show winding-to-core insulation degraded to ≤360 kΩ (lowest 400 Ω); one unit passed 500 V hi-pot but broke down at 800 V (spec: 1600 VAC) |
| E3 | Physical threshold | Wire-to-core sees ~170–373 V PWM steps at high line, exceeding the minimum gas-discharge ignition voltage (~330 V, Paschen); at low line ≤170 V ignition is **physically impossible** — matching the binary 17:1 selectivity |
| E4 | Time scale | ~10⁵ PWM edges/s × enamel PD endurance of 10⁹–10¹⁰ discharges = **3–30 h**, matching ≤5 h field failures. Purely thermal failure of intact enamel would require ~240 °C hotspots (Arrhenius) and mass charring — contradicted by visually normal windings; pure thermal aging is excluded |
| E5 | Humidity gradient | Within high-voltage regions, failure rates order monotonically with climate humidity (SG>CN>HK>KR>dry regions at zero), consistent with discharge/tracking physics (shipment-timing normalization in progress) |
| E6 | Geometry | In the 6-slot concentrated winding, every slot hosts two different phases plus the core; slot edges concentrate winding-process mechanical stress — consistent with the observed single-coil loss and multi-phase ground degradation |

### 4.3 Independent secondary issue (managed separately)

Four units built on Apr 1 show "open circuit + intact ground insulation + normal winding" — a **lead-termination/weld process event** (date-clustered); supplier process audit in progress.

### 4.4 Exclusions

Bearings (loose in only 1/18); IPM IC lot (failures across ≥6 lots); pure thermal aging (E4); shoot-through as primary (1/17); grid surge / broken-neutral events (no MOV damage or multi-component carbonization signatures).
